  ### 3. Backend Authorization & Operational Safeguards

  In serverPlatformRoutes.js:1307-1410:

  • Authentication Middleware (authenticatePlatformStaff):
      • Resolves staff identity via X-Platform-User-Id header, Authorization: Bearer platform-<userId>.<version>, or
      active simulation actor.
      • Rejects suspended staff with 403 ACCOUNT_SUSPENDED.
      • Rejects pending invitations with 403 INVITATION_PENDING.
      • Rejects expired credentials with 403 ACCESS_EXPIRED.
      • Rejects stale tokens where tokenVersion !== user.session_version with 401 SESSION_REVOKED.
  • Tenant Scope Enforcement (requireTenantScope):
      • Automatically filters tenant lists and blocks access to /api/platform/tenants/:id/* and /api/platform/support-
      sessions/ for unassigned companies with 403 TENANT_OUT_OF_SCOPE.
  • Privilege Escalation Protection (validateRoleAssignment):
      • Prevents non-Super Admins from assigning super_admin or granting roles with rank ≥ their own.
      • Prevents non-Super Admins from assigning companies outside their own assigned scope.
  • Last Super Admin Protection (isLastActiveSuperAdmin):
      • Guard rejects suspension or demotion of the last active Super Admin with 400 LAST_SUPER_ADMIN_PROTECTED.
  • Session Revocation:
      • Incrementing session_version whenever an account is suspended, role is downgraded, or company scope is
      modified.
  • Immutable Audit Logging:
      • Logs PLATFORM_USER_CREATED, PLATFORM_USER_INVITED, PLATFORM_USER_ROLE_SCOPE_MODIFIED, PLATFORM_USER_SUSPENDED,
      and PLATFORM_USER_REACTIVATED with actor email, target user ID, reason, and change diffs.
